Our Core Programs

Expert-led courses to take you from concept to creation.

Fundamentals of LLM

Master the foundations of modern AI.

Learn the core ideas behind large language models, from attention mechanisms and transformer architectures that power ChatGPT and Gemini to fine-tuning and inference. Understand how LLMs reason, generate text, and drive real-world applications such as chatbots, assistants, and creative tools.

Agentic AI

Build intelligent systems that think and act.

Explore how to design and deploy AI agents that can reason, plan, and collaborate to achieve complex goals. Gain practical experience with frameworks for memory-driven workflows, tool use, and multi-agent coordination while learning safe and transparent decision-making techniques.

Real World Internship Experience

Apply your skills to real business challenges.

Work on live AI projects that mirror industry use cases. Experience the full development cycle from data preparation and model training to product deployment and client presentation, guided by mentors who bring real-world perspective and expertise.
